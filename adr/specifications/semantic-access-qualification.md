`ganesh/fai-645-policy-evidence-closure` at
`3d7a2e67cecf5ab75996f7a49a91cfaed6da8c56` (2026-09-06).

for the full wording of the row. `PARTIAL` identifies an implemented slice
whose full profile path is not qualified. `FAIL` identifies a missing or
contradictory implementation. `NOT APPLICABLE` is reserved for a requirement
outside this profile.

## Remaining dependency boundaries

`STR-08` and the second clause of `ENF-06` fail because standalone DataPolicy
is deliberately retained until FAI-649. They are cutover-owned limitations,
not instructions to remove DataPolicy in this layer. The other failing rows
are candidate registry resolution (`ATT-06`), registered-type input to the
existing compatibility classifier (`CMP-02`), and complete durable semantic
evaluation evidence (`LIF-06`). Generic query/access audit already exists.

Partial rows preserve the remaining provider/expiry, every-consumer and plan
shape, diagnostics/audit, and activation/approval evidence boundaries. Unsupported
protected substitution is rejected; rejection is not claimed as positive
rollup support. Read-through cache fencing does not require a new event transport.

FAI-648 remains **IMPLEMENTED / PARTIAL** (Linear: In Progress). This layer does
not upgrade FAI-617 or FAI-641, and does not invalidate FAI-662's qualified
publication-integrity boundary. FAI-649 still depends on qualified upstream
consumer/control evidence and FAI-616; FAI-632 cannot close active ADR scope
before that chain and the cutover evidence are complete. Neither issue is
started here.
